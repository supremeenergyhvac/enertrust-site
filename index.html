How this is calculated

Fully amortizing loan, interest compounded semi-annually and paid monthly (the usual Canadian mortgage method). No lender, legal or appraisal fees included. Your lender may calculate differently.

monthly rate r = (1 + annual rate ÷ 2)^(1/6) − 1 payment = amount × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−(years × 12)) management = 8% × rent (HST extra, if applicable) left for you = rent − payment − management − HST − allowance

Straight answers

What homeowners ask before they book.

The assessment is free and there's no obligation.

Will I know exactly what I'll make before we start?

Yes. Before any work begins, your agreement states your guaranteed monthly rent, the 8% management fee and your financing payment, so you know your monthly number in writing.

What does this actually cost me each month?

You make one monthly financing payment to your lender, about $960 in our example, and the rent is designed to cover it plus management. In the example, about $696 a month is left for a one-bedroom and $880 for a two-bedroom, before HST, other ownership costs and income tax.

Will I owe capital gains tax when I sell?

Your home stays your principal residence. Canadian tax rules can treat the rented portion separately, so only part of a future gain may be taxable, depending on how the suite is set up and whether depreciation is claimed. Your accountant can confirm for your situation, and we can introduce one.

Will my property tax or insurance go up?

You already pay property tax and home insurance on your home. Insurance usually rises somewhat when you add a rental unit, and a reassessment may change your property tax. Your assessment includes these in your monthly picture.

Will my basement work?

The assessment checks ceiling height, structure, drainage, access and existing services, then recommends a layout. If it isn't suitable, we tell you before you spend anything.

When does the 60-day build clock start?

The day your project deposit is received, as written in your agreement. If the suite isn't finished in 60 days, EnerTrust pays you the agreed monthly rent until it is.

How long is the rent guaranteed?

For the first 12 months, starting when your suite is approved for occupancy. If we're still finding your tenant, or a tenant falls behind, we cover the gap, so your agreed rent still arrives. It applies to eligible suites with the EnerTrust finish package and our ongoing management. After year one, we keep managing the suite and normal rental risks apply.

Will the suite hold up to tenants?

The finish package is chosen for durability under tenant use and turnover, and our team walks through the suite every spring and fall to catch small issues early.

Do Ontario's housing changes guarantee a permit?

No. Zoning, Building Code, fire safety, servicing and inspection rules still apply, and the municipality decides. We prepare and file the application.

Will a legal suite add value to my home?

It turns unfinished space into finished, legal, income-producing space, which may support a higher appraisal. The amount depends on your home, the market and an independent appraisal, and an increase isn't guaranteed.

Where in Ontario do you work?

Requests come from across Ontario. Availability for your location is confirmed during the assessment.
