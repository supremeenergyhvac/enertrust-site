Privacy policy

Enertrust Inc. · Last updated November 25, 2026

This policy explains how Enertrust Inc. ("EnerTrust", "we") handles personal information collected through enertrust.ca. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

When you request an assessment, we collect your name, phone number and email address, and any details you choose to share about your home. Our site and form provider also record basic technical information, such as the page you came from, your browser type and your IP address.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your assessment request and answer your questions.
  • To prepare an assessment or proposal for your property.
  • To send you follow-up information about our basement suite program. You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in each email, or by asking us.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run our business, including our customer management and form system (HighLevel), our website host (GitHub Pages) and our email tools. They handle your information only to provide services to us. If you move forward with a project, we may share the information needed with partners involved in it, such as the lender you choose, contractors and the team that manages your suite. Some providers store data outside Canada, where it may be subject to local laws.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent to being contacted. Email us at enertrust04@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

Our website itself does not use advertising cookies. Our form provider may use cookies or similar technology to prevent spam and to tell us how you found us.

Contact

Questions about this policy: enertrust04@gmail.com.
